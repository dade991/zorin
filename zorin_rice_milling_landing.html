Our Process

From Paddy Field to Your Table

Every grain passes through our rigorous 4-stage process before it earns the Zorin name.

01
Sourcing & Harvesting
Direct procurement from certified partner farms. We monitor every harvest for consistency and quality.
02
Cleaning & Drying
State-of-the-art drying systems reduce moisture to optimal levels without compromising nutritional value.
03
Precision Milling
German-engineered milling equipment calibrated for maximum grain integrity and minimal breakage.
04
Grading & Packing
Each batch is laser-sorted, lab-tested and packed in sealed moisture-barrier packaging for freshness.

FAQs

Got Questions? We Have Got You Covered.

What kinds of farming solutions do you offer?
We provide end-to-end milling, grading, storage and distribution solutions. We also offer agronomic consulting for partner farms focused on yield and sustainability improvement.
How can I start using the Zorin platform?
Simply complete our distributor registration form online. Our team will review your application and schedule an onboarding call within 48 hours.
Are your technologies eco-friendly?
Yes. All our milling facilities run on a combination of solar and biomass energy. Rice husk waste is converted to fuel, producing near-zero landfill from our operations.
Do you provide support and training?
All distribution partners receive dedicated account management, quarterly product training sessions and 24/7 access to our digital support portal.
What minimum order quantities apply?
Wholesale orders start from 1 metric tonne. Export orders from 5 metric tonnes. Retail packaging orders have separate MOQs — contact our sales team for a detailed quote.
